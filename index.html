✨ Particle Playground Controls

Control particles using your camera gestures or manual fallback controls.

🖐️ Hand Gestures

  • Spread: Move hands apart or together to expand/contract particle cloud.
  • Scale: Open hand (fingers spread) to enlarge, close hand into a fist to shrink.
  • Fireworks: Make a fast snap-fist gesture while using the Fireworks template.

🖱️ Manual Fallback

If camera is disabled, uncheck Enable Camera Control in the control panel to use mouse drag.
